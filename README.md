## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Kdenlive** is an open-source, non-linear video editor designed for Windows that provides a wealth of features for both beginners and professionals alike. With the **latest version**, users can take advantage of multiple timelines, a variety of basic effects, and seamless transitions that make video editing a breeze. 

Whether you're creating simple video projects or complex films, **Kdenlive** offers a comprehensive feature set that allows full functionality for all your editing needs. ## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Multi-Channel Editing** — Utilize several audio and video channels simultaneously, easily organized for your editing needs.
- **2D Title Creation Tools** — Create stunning titles with various alignment options, fonts, colors, shadows, and rotation effects.
- **Wide Range of Effects** — Apply color correction, transformations, and visual filters to enhance your videos.
- **Audio & Video Analysis** — Access tools like Audio Meter, Waveform, and Vectorscope for detailed monitoring.
- **Automatic Backups** — Ensure your work is safe with low-resolution backups and scheduled backups every five minutes.

### Advanced Features:
- **Customizable Interface** — Choose from several preset themes and download additional resources such as rendering settings and effects.
- **Cross-Computer Editing** — Automatically create backups to edit your projects on different devices without hassle.
